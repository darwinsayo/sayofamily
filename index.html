Faith, Family & Hands-On Living

We are a bustling family of ten living in Harrison County, Kentucky. Our days are filled with the wonderful chaos of homeschooling eight children, raising backyard chickens, tending to DIY homestead improvements, and building everything from wood crafts to digital projects.

Whether it’s woodworking in the workshop, fixing up vehicles, capturing memories through photography, or exploring the great outdoors, there's never a dull moment under our roof.
