Our Story

Ten lives intertwined under one roof, guided by faith and a love for building things that last.

Founded in 2006, our family has grown into a bustling, vibrant household of ten. From workshops and cameras to schoolbooks and backyard chicken coops, our days are never quiet and never dull.

Whether we’re tackling home renovation projects, exploring the outdoors, or sharing a meal around a crowded table, we cherish the chaos, the quiet moments, and the journey we share together every single day.
